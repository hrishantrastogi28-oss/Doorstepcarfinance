Privacy Policy: Doorstepcarloan

Effective date: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

Doorstepcarloan (also known as Door Step Car Sales & Finance), operated by Doorstepcarsales, is a car loan and car finance facilitation service serving customers in Lucknow, nearby towns, and Bengaluru, India. We help customers prepare documents and apply for car loans with partner banks and financial institutions. We are a loan facilitator, not a bank, and loan approval, interest rates and terms are decided by the lending bank.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, phone number, city and, if you choose to share it, your email address.
  • Details about the car you want to buy or finance and the loan amount you need.
  • Any message you send us through our enquiry form, phone, WhatsApp or email.

Information needed to process a loan application (only after you decide to proceed)

  • Identity and address proof, such as Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence or voter ID.
  • Income documents, bank statements, employment or business proof, and photographs.
  • Vehicle documents, such as RC, insurance and invoice.
  • Any other documents a bank or lender asks for.

We ask only for what the lender needs to process your application.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as your IP address, device type, browser, pages viewed and time spent on our website.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, including those used by Google (for example, Google Ads and Google tag), to measure visits and the performance of our advertising.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and call or message you about your car loan request.
  • Prepare and submit your loan application to banks or financial institutions you agree to.
  • Follow up with lenders and keep you updated until your loan is processed.
  • Run and improve our website, advertising and services.
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share your information with

We share your information only as needed to provide our service:

  • Banks and financial institutions that you agree to apply with, so they can assess your loan.
  • Service providers who help us run the website, send messages, store data or measure advertising (for example, Google and WhatsApp).
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order or a regulator.

Partner banks and lenders handle your data under their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising and cookies

We use Google Ads and similar tools to show our ads and measure whether they work. These tools may use cookies to recognise your browser. You can control cookies in your browser settings. You can also manage how Google uses data for ads through your Google account’s ad settings. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of our website work.

6. WhatsApp and phone communication

If you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, or give us your number in our form, you agree that we may contact you about your enquiry and your loan application. If you do not want to be contacted, tell us and we will stop.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and loan application, and for as long as the law or lender requirements say we must. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting access to the people who need it and using reasonably secure systems. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive documents through unsecured channels you do not trust.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Stop contacting you.
  • Withdraw your consent for how we use your information.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 1. We will respond within a reasonable time. We follow applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as they apply to us.

10. Children

Our services are meant for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
