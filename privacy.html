Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

When you submit the enquiry form, we collect the name, phone number, city, car type, approximate loan amount and any message you provide. We use these details to respond to your enquiry and help with car finance.

How information is handled

Enquiry form submissions are stored in our Supabase-hosted database. The public website can submit an enquiry but cannot view stored enquiries. We retain enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and manage our business. You may also choose to open WhatsApp after submitting; WhatsApp does not send a message until you tap Send.

We do not sell personal information. We may share details with a lending bank when needed to review an application and with your knowledge. Supabase, phone, email, WhatsApp, hosting and analytics providers apply their own privacy terms.

Your choices

To ask about correcting or deleting information you shared, email Doorstepcarsales@gmail.com.
