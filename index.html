Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Call or message us and we will explain it simply.

Can I get a loan on a used car?

It may be available; eligibility, vehicle age and amount depend on the bank and application.

How much loan can I get?

It depends on income, credit profile, car value and the lender’s rules.

How long does approval take?

Timing varies by bank and document readiness. We keep you updated.

What documents are needed?

Usually ID, address proof, PAN, income proof or bank statement and photo. Used cars may need RC and insurance.

Is there any charge for your service?

We explain applicable service or bank charges before you proceed.

Can I get a loan with a low credit score?

The lender decides based on the full application. Ask us about your options.

Can I transfer my existing car loan?

Ask us about a balance transfer; the lender reviews eligibility.
